About TV Activation

What is TV Activation and Why Do You Need It?

TV activation is the process of connecting your television, set-top box, or streaming device to your subscription account so the service provider recognises your device as authorised to receive the full channel lineup. Without a completed activation, your device will show only preview content, display error codes, or loop through an activation prompt indefinitely.

Modern TV services — whether satellite, cable, or streaming — use activation codes and device pairing to protect content licensing and ensure only paying subscribers access the full service. The process is designed to be simple, but it frequently becomes complicated with new devices, account transfers, subscription lapses, or system resets.

ClearLink exists to make this simple. We handle the full activation process, troubleshoot when things go wrong, and make sure every device in your home works exactly as it should.

📺 Just got a new TV or streaming device? Every new device must be individually activated — even if your subscription is active on other devices. Contact us and we'll walk you through it in minutes.
