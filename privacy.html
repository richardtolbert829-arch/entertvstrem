Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

ClearLink is an independent TV activation and information resource. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What We Collect

  • Contact form submissions — name, email, and message content
  • Analytics data — pages visited, session duration, browser type
  • Cookies — for site functionality and analytics

We do not collect payment information or TV account credentials. We cannot access subscriber databases for any TV provider.

How We Use It

  • To respond to questions and activation enquiries via the contact form
  • To understand site usage and improve our content

Cookies

We use analytics cookies. Manage preferences via your browser settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with our hosting provider under appropriate agreements.

Your Rights

Email privacy@clearlink.com to access, correct, or delete your data. We respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
