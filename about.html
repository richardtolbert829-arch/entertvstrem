Who We Are

An Independent TV Activation Resource

ClearLink is an independent information and activation resource for TV subscribers — whether you use a satellite service, a streaming platform, a cable package, or a combination of all three. We are not affiliated with any TV provider, streaming network, or broadcaster. We exist to make TV activation and account management significantly less frustrating than it can be when you're trying to navigate provider support on your own.

Our team understands TV systems from the technical level — how activation codes work, how devices get authorised, what causes error codes, how subscription tiers differ, and what the fastest path to resolution is for every common TV problem. We apply that knowledge directly to help you get your TV working and keep it working.

Our information is free, honest, and uninfluenced by any commercial relationship with a TV provider. This is an independent website — we are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting as a representative of any TV provider.
